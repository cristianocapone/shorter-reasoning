**Shortest replay has the highest point estimate of three replay rules in every setting; on mathematics an accounting split places its advantage in finishing within the token cap.** **a**, Identical copies of each model are fine-tuned (LoRA rank 32, 1 epoch) on one of its own verified solutions per training problem: the shortest (S), a random one (R) or the longest (L). The arms differ only in the selection rule, so the replayed solutions differ in length and also in content; S2 repeats shortest replay starting from S; the untouched model is the control. **b**, Difference from the untouched model on ARC-AGI-2 (Qwen3.8-27B agent, 72 puzzles, 5 runs per arm; puzzles × 100/72 gives percentage points), MATH-500 (Qwen3-4B, 5 seeds per arm and 3 for S2; DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B, 3 seeds per arm) and APPS (Qwen3-4B, 3 seeds per arm). Dots: mean differences, filled when the paired-bootstrap 95% interval over problems (bar) excludes 0; P values are two-sided. The banner compares shortest replay with the untouched agent (+4.2 puzzles; P = 0.003 over puzzles, 0.15 over runs, exact permutation test on the run totals; tokens per puzzle, −4.5%); against random and longest replay the ARC difference is +1.8 puzzles (P = 0.25 and 0.29). **c**, Accounting split. With F the share of samples that finish within the cap (8k tokens) and Q = A/F the accuracy per finished sample, accuracy is A = F × Q, and the difference between shortest and random or longest replay splits exactly into a finishing term, ΔF × mean Q, and a quality term, ΔQ × mean F (Kitagawa's decomposition; means over the two arms; intervals: those of ΔF and ΔQ times the same factors): on MATH the finishing term carries the gain. ARC rows: the agent's message logs (24k-token cap per message; the untouched agent's logs cover 3 of its 5 runs: 61.3% cut, 54.9k tokens per code-carrying message; shortest vs untouched P = 0.19 and 0.12); 'cut at the cap' counts messages cut while still reasoning; tokens per code-carrying message is the restart cost E[min(L, t)] / P(code).
