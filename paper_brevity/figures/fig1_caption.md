**Design and per-seed result on ARC-AGI-2.** **a**, For each training problem one of the model's own verified solutions is kept by one of three rules (shortest, random, longest), and three copies of the model are fine-tuned with the same recipe; only the replayed reasoning differs. **b**, ARC-AGI-2 puzzles solved (of 72) by the Qwen3.8-27B program-writing agent, 5 seeds per arm (dots: seeds; bars: means, 15.4 untouched, 17.8 longest, 17.8 random, 19.6 shortest). Shortest replay has the highest mean and solves 4.2 more puzzles than the untouched model (paired bootstrap over puzzles, 95% interval 1.2 to 7.4, two-sided P = 0.003; exact permutation test on the run totals, P = 0.15); its seeds span 17–21 puzzles, those of the untouched model 10–22. Its lead over random and longest replay is not resolved (random replay +1.8 puzzles, P = 0.25, longest replay +1.8 puzzles, P = 0.29).
