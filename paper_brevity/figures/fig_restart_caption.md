**Loosening the token cap reduces the advantage of shortest over longest replay to a non-significant 0.17 points but not its brevity.** If the cap t on a sample acts as a restart cutoff, the expected cost of a solution is E[min(L, t)] / P(L ≤ t) (Luby, Sinclair & Zuckerman, 1993), so replaying short solutions, which shifts the length distribution L to the left, should pay only while the cap binds; any account in which the gain works through finishing within the cap predicts the same.
